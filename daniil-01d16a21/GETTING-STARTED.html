Слабший комп'ютер? Перші тижні підуть і на скромнішій відеокарті з налаштуваннями Medium. Для важких тижнів (7–12) є варіанти:

🏝️ Game Asset Development with Blender and Unreal: 12 weeks

Every day you learn with your own free AI tutor (Gemini or ChatGPT), then you build for real. In 12 weeks: from your first prop in Blender to your own island with two finished districts and a flight over the whole island. You start from zero, and no programming is needed.

⚠️ The course lives in a shared Google Drive folder. Install Google Drive for desktop, find the course folder (section 4) and open the day pages from the synced folder on your own PC by double-clicking: they open in your browser. Don't open them on drive.google.com or with Google Docs: the Copy buttons don't work there.

🧭 First time signing up or installing? Open ACCOUNTS-AND-INSTALLS.html: it walks you step by step through every account (Google, ChatGPT, Epic, Fab), every install, and what to do if something goes wrong.

1. What's ahead

The course runs from Day 0 to Day 60: Monday to Friday, weekends free. Day 0 is accounts, installs, a first hour in Blender and your Unreal project IslandCourse. Then 12 weeks:

  • Week 1 (Days 1 to 5): Blender from zero: props at real size.
  • Week 2 (Days 6 to 10): modular kits.
  • Week 3 (Days 11 to 15): surfaces: UVs, materials, textures.
  • Week 4 (Days 16 to 20): the Unreal look: colour, textures, decals, light.
  • Week 5 (Days 21 to 25): your real place and your island on paper.
  • Week 6 (Days 26 to 30): terrain and water.
  • Week 7 (Days 31 to 35): roads, the big world and a first forest.
  • Week 8 (Days 36 to 40): PCG nature for the whole island.
  • Week 9 (Days 41 to 45): district 1: the waterfront.
  • Week 10 (Days 46 to 50): district 1 finished, district 2 built.
  • Week 11 (Days 51 to 55): district 2 finished.
  • Week 12 (Days 56 to 60): the whole island, polished, flown and presented.

🏁 The finish line: your own island (about 1 km) grounded in a real coastal place, with two finished districts of about 200 x 200 m each and a 2-minute flight over the whole island.

Every Friday (Day 5, 10, … 60) is 🏆 Boss day: a timed build with Bronze, Silver and Gold tiers.

2. Your PC

SystemWindows 10 or 11
Graphics cardRTX 4060 class (8 GB video memory) or better
Memory16 GB minimum; 32 GB recommended from week 7 (with 16 GB, only Electric Dreams' small levels work)
DiskA fast SSD with at least 250 GB free for the whole course: Unreal Engine 5.8 (about 40 GB), Electric Dreams (about 57 to 60 GB to download; keep three times that free, because shaders and derived data grow) and your projects

A weaker PC? The first weeks run on a modest graphics card at Medium settings. For the heavy weeks (7 to 12) you have options:

  • with 16 GB of memory: raise the Windows page file to 32 GB or more, close the browser while shaders compile, and keep Scalability at High;
  • set Scalability to Medium or Low and open only the small levels the day page recommends;
  • stream from a stronger PC (yours or a friend's) with Parsec or Sunshine + Moonlight: Unreal runs there and you see its screen on yours;
  • rent a cloud PC (for example Shadow or Vagon). Prices change: check them on the service's own site before paying.

3. What to install (all free)

  1. Blender (the latest stable 5.x): blender.org
  2. Krita for painting and photos: krita.org (the free version is from there, not from the stores). GIMP, Inkscape or Photopea in the browser also work.
  3. The Epic Games Launcher and in it Unreal Engine 5.8 (about 40 GB; start the download the evening before). Your Epic account also opens Fab and its free packs.
  4. A free Gemini or ChatGPT account: that's your tutor.

  6. From Day 24: Epic's free Electric Dreams sample on Fab, a separate project called ElectricDreamsSample (about 57 to 60 GB). You start the download on Day 24 and start working with it on Day 34.
  7. From Day 24: free packs from Fab. Another free source is allowed too: Poly Haven.

🛠️ On Day 0 you create your one Unreal project, IslandCourse (Third Person template, Water plugin). Step by step: ACCOUNTS-AND-INSTALLS.html, section 5.

4. Handing in and feedback: Google Drive

The course folder is shared with you on Google Drive. Everything you save in your Work folder reaches the course coordinator automatically, and the feedback comes back to you the same way.

  1. Install Google Drive for desktop: google.com/drive/download. Run the installer and sign in with the Google account that has access to the course folder.
  2. Find the shared folder: in your browser open drive.google.com → Shared with me → right-click the course folder → Organize → Add shortcut to Drive → My Drive → Add. Only after this does the folder sync to your PC.
  3. Open it on your PC: File Explorer → the Google Drive drive → My Drive → the course folder. Open the pages from there by double-clicking (COURSE.html first). Never open them on drive.google.com or in Google Docs: the Copy buttons fail there.
  4. Hand in every day, into that day's folder: save your files, screenshots, the 60-second clip and summary.md in Work\Assignment\Day-NN-…\. It syncs automatically: there is nothing to send. Before you shut the PC down, wait until the Google Drive icon near the clock says syncing is finished.
  5. Big Unreal projects stay out of the synced folder. IslandCourse and ElectricDreamsSample live on a fast local drive (at least 250 GB free for everything), never inside Work or Google Drive. Work holds only what you hand in.
  6. Feedback arrives as THALIA-COMMENTS.md in that day's folder (Work\Assignment\Day-NN-…\): it is feedback from Thalia, the course coordinator. Open it with Notepad and look for it before the next day starts.

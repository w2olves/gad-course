4. Здача робіт, відгук і питання

Курс ти читаєш онлайн, а Google Drive потрібен лише для здачі. Ти працюєш у робочій папці дня на своєму ПК, а наприкінці дня перетягуєш готові файли в папку здачі цього дня на Google Drive. Талія, координаторка курсу, повертає відгук у ту саму папку здачі. Здача щодня однакова.

Дві папки дня

Робоча папка дняНа твоєму ПК: <диск>\GAD\Day-NN-…, наприклад D:\GAD\Day-01-bollard. Тут ти працюєш і зберігаєш усе за день.
Папка здачіНа Google Drive: Work → Assignment → папка цього дня, у ній лежить HAND-IN-HERE.txt. Сюди ти лише перетягуєш готові файли.

Як здавати

  1. Щоранку створи робочу папку дня. Папка GAD лежить на твоєму найшвидшому диску (часто D:), поруч із папкою Unreal: її створюєш у День 0. Перед першим уроком створи в GAD папку з такою самою назвою, як папка здачі цього дня: <диск>\GAD\Day-NN-…, наприклад D:\GAD\Day-01-bollard. Сюди зберігаєш .blend, експорти, фото й скриншоти.
  2. Спершу звіт дня. Наприкінці дня натисни «Копіювати звіт дня», встав у новий чат і відповідай тьютору. Відповідь збережи в summary.md у робочій папці дня. Файл уже є? Відкрий його в Блокноті, натисни Ctrl+End, встав відповідь і натисни Ctrl+S. Нічого з написаного раніше не стирай.
  3. Відкрий папку здачі. Угорі сторінки дня, на картці «Що здаєш сьогодні», натисни «Відкрити мою папку здачі». На Google Drive відкриється папка здачі саме цього дня. Ти в правильному місці, якщо в ній лежить HAND-IN-HERE.txt.
  4. Перетягни файли. Виділи в Провіднику (File Explorer) усі файли зі списку «Що здати», разом із summary.md, і перетягни їх у вікно браузера. Лише файли: ніколи не створюй і не завантажуй папки, особливо Work, Assignment чи Day-…. Інакше Google Drive тримає дві папки з однаковою назвою, і твоя робота губиться.
  5. Перевір. Кожна назва зі списку «Що здати» є в папці. У колонці «Розмір файлу» (File size) кліп менший за 50 МБ. Файл .blend чи .glb розміром лише кілька КБ зазвичай порожній: відкрий його на ПК, виправ і перетягни ще раз.
  6. Виправляєш файл? Перетягни його ще раз з тією самою назвою: Google Drive збереже нову версію того самого файлу. Не обирай варіант зі збереженням обох файлів (Keep both files).

Проєкти Unreal (IslandCourse, ElectricDreamsSample) ніколи не здаються й не лежать на Google Drive.

Що здавати

Щодня список «Що здати» має не більше 4 рядків, а в День боса — не більше 6. Один рядок може назвати кілька файлів; усі файли дня названо на картці «Що здаєш сьогодні». Рядки бувають такі:

  1. summary.md (звіт дня);
  2. файл, який відкриє пізніший день, з позначкою «(→ День N)»;
  3. один скриншот-доказ, що мету дня досягнуто, з позначкою «(доказ)»;
  4. файл нотаток — лише якщо він знадобиться пізніше (→ День N).

🏆 У День боса додай скриншоти боса, свою таблицю оцінювання й один кліп на 30–60 секунд, 1080p, менше 50 МБ (Win+Alt+R). Збережи кліп і на ПК: з кліпів складеш ролик прогресу в День 60. Кліп більший? Відкрий його в Clipchamp (є у Windows 11) → Export → 720p: 720p теж приймається.

Усе інше, що ти робиш (тренувальні файли, експорти, пробні скриншоти), лишається в робочій папці дня на ПК. Здавати це не треба.

⛔ Ніколи не здавай папку проєкту чи рівня Unreal, кліп понад 50 МБ або копію папки.

Тримай їх у папці GAD на ПК, на рівень вище за робочу папку дня, і доповнюй по ходу. Коли список «Що здати» їх називає, перетягни їх у папку здачі цього дня, як усі інші файли.

🏝️ Game Asset Development with Blender and Unreal: 12 weeks

Every day you learn with your own free AI tutor (Gemini or ChatGPT), then you build for real. In 12 weeks: from your first model in Blender to your own island with two finished districts and a flythrough over the whole island. You start from zero, and no programming is needed.

⚠️ The course opens online at the personal link in your email. Bookmark it (Ctrl+D) and open it every day in the same browser on the same PC: that is where your progress is saved. Your Google Drive folder is only for handing in work (section 4). Don't open course pages from Google Drive: the Copy buttons don't work there.

🧭 Before Day 0, do the Before Day 0 list. It covers the accounts (Google, Gemini or ChatGPT, Epic and Fab) and the programs (Unreal Engine 5.8, Blender, Krita): about 1 hour 30 minutes, plus the long Unreal download. Step by step, with tips for a weak PC and an unusual keyboard: ACCOUNTS-AND-INSTALLS.html.

1. What's ahead

The course runs from Day 0 to Day 60: Monday to Friday, weekends free. Before Day 0 you make your accounts and install the programs from the Before Day 0 list. Day 0 itself (about 4 hours) is your folders, a first hour in Blender and your Unreal project IslandCourse. Then 12 weeks:

  • Week 1 (Days 1 to 5): Blender from zero: props at real size.
  • Week 2 (Days 6 to 10): modular kits.
  • Week 3 (Days 11 to 15): surfaces: UVs, materials, textures.
  • Week 4 (Days 16 to 20): the Unreal look: colour, textures, decals, light.
  • Week 5 (Days 21 to 25): your real place and your island on paper.
  • Week 6 (Days 26 to 30): terrain and water.
  • Week 7 (Days 31 to 35): roads, the big world and a first forest.
  • Week 8 (Days 36 to 40): PCG nature for the whole island.
  • Week 9 (Days 41 to 45): district 1: the waterfront.
  • Week 10 (Days 46 to 50): district 1 finished, district 2 built.
  • Week 11 (Days 51 to 55): district 2 finished.
  • Week 12 (Days 56 to 60): the whole island, polished, flown and presented.

🏁 The finish line: your own island (about 1 km) grounded in a real coastal place, with two finished districts of about 200 × 200 m each and a 2-minute flythrough over the whole island.

Every Friday (Day 5, 10, … 60) is 🏆 Boss day: a timed build with Bronze, Silver and Gold medals. Boss-day lessons are short, about 20 minutes each, and the build takes about 4 hours.

A weaker PC? You can do the course in Unreal 5.8 even on a laptop with a 4 GB graphics card (GTX 1650 class) and 16 GB of memory. You change a few settings for yourself only, and the project stays as the course sets it up. Step by step: ACCOUNTS-AND-INSTALLS, section 16. In short:

  • work at Scalability Medium (Settings → Engine Scalability Settings) and lower Screen Percentage to 50 to 75%;
  • with 16 GB of memory, on Day 24 raise the Windows page file to 32 GB or more, and close the browser while shaders compile;
  • with a graphics card of 4 GB or less, you take the light route on Day 34, without Electric Dreams;
  • don't switch to Unreal 4.27: it lacks many of the tools the course teaches;
  • stream from a stronger PC (yours or a friend's) with Parsec or Sunshine + Moonlight: Unreal runs there and you see its screen on yours;
  • rent a cloud PC (for example Shadow or Vagon). Prices change: check them on the service's own site before paying.

4. Handing in, feedback and questions

You read the course online; Google Drive is only for handing in. You work in your day work folder on your PC, and at the end of the day you drag your finished files into that day's hand-in folder on Google Drive. Thalia, the course coordinator, puts her feedback in the same hand-in folder. Handing in works the same way every day.

The day's two folders

Day work folderOn your PC: <drive>\GAD\Day-NN-…, for example D:\GAD\Day-01-bollard. You work here and save everything for the day.
Hand-in folderOn Google Drive: Work → Assignment → that day's folder, with HAND-IN-HERE.txt in it. You only drag finished files in here.

How to hand in

  1. Each morning, make your day work folder. Your GAD folder is on your fastest drive (often D:), beside your Unreal folder: you make it on Day 0. Before the first lesson, make a folder in GAD with the same name as that day's hand-in folder: <drive>\GAD\Day-NN-…, such as D:\GAD\Day-01-bollard. Save your .blend files, exports, photos and screenshots there.
  2. The day report first. At the end of the day, click "Copy the day report", paste it into a new chat and answer the tutor. Save the answer as summary.md in your day work folder. The file already exists? Open it in Notepad, press Ctrl+End, paste the answer and press Ctrl+S. Never delete what is already there.
  3. Open your hand-in folder. At the top of the day page, on the "What you hand in today" card, click "Open my hand-in folder". That day's hand-in folder opens on Google Drive. You're in the right place if you see HAND-IN-HERE.txt in it.
  4. Drag in the files. In File Explorer, select all the files on the "Hand in" list, summary.md included, and drag them into the browser window. Files only: never create or upload a folder, especially not Work, Assignment or Day-…. Otherwise Google Drive keeps two folders with the same name, and your work gets lost.
  5. Check. Every name on the "Hand in" list shows in the folder. The File size column shows the clip under 50 MB. A .blend or .glb file of only a few KB is usually empty: open it on your PC, fix it and drag it in again.
  6. Fixing a file? Drag it in again with the same name: Google Drive saves it as a new version of the same file. Don't choose Keep both files.

Unreal projects (IslandCourse, ElectricDreamsSample) are never handed in and never go on Google Drive.

What to hand in

Each day the "Hand in" list has at most 4 lines, 6 on a Boss day. One line may name several files; the "What you hand in today" card names every file for the day. The lines are:

  1. summary.md, the day report;
  2. the file a later day opens, marked "(→ Day N)";
  3. one proof screenshot that shows today's goal is met, marked "(proof)";
  4. a notes file, only when a later day uses it (→ Day N).

🏆 On a Boss day, add the Boss screenshots, your rubric and one clip of 30 to 60 seconds, 1080p, under 50 MB (Win+Alt+R). Keep the clip on your PC too: on Day 60 you cut your clips into a progress reel. Clip too big? Open it in Clipchamp (built into Windows 11) → Export → 720p: 720p is accepted too.

Everything else you make (practice files, exports, test screenshots) stays in your day work folder on your PC. You don't hand it in.

⛔ Never hand in an Unreal project or level folder, a clip over 50 MB, or a copy of a folder.

📒 Two files run for the whole course: credits.md (the source and licence of every photo, map and pack) and kit_rules.md (your kit's rules). Keep them in your GAD folder on your PC, one level above your day work folder, and add to them as you go. When the "Hand in" list names them, drag them into that day's hand-in folder like any other file.

Feedback, score and questions

  • Feedback from Thalia appears in that day's hand-in folder, as THALIA-COMMENTS.md. Google Drive opens it right in the browser. Look for it before the next day.
  • Your score out of 10 appears on the day page and on the course map.
  • A question? Write it on the "Question for Thalia" line of the report for the day you're working on, even if it's about another day. Start with the day number: "Day 0: …". The answer appears in THALIA-COMMENTS.md in that day's hand-in folder.
  • Something blocks you? Reply to Thalia's email with the day number, what you tried and a screenshot.

6. How a day works

  1. 🎯 In the morning: make your day work folder (section 4) and read the mission: what you build today, its spec and what you hand in (the "What you hand in today" card at the top). The "Comes back on" line shows the later day where your work is used again.
  2. 📘 Lessons of 40 to 50 minutes: click Copy lesson, open a new chat in Gemini or ChatGPT, paste (Ctrl+V) and send. The tutor asks whether you've done this before; only if you have does it ask 3 short questions, and if you answer them right you go straight to the practice. Then you do the task step by step, and the tutor explains each new thing where you first need it and checks your screenshots. It ends with one question and a two-line lesson summary: "Learned: …" and "Hard: …". Paste it into the box under the lesson, then click Done.
  3. 🏗️ Build on your own, about 3 hours 30 minutes (about 4 hours on a Boss day): follow the steps on the day page. The first step checks that your lesson files are in your day work folder. Skipped a lesson? Do its "with your tutor" steps first (about 25 minutes). Stuck? Click Copy the build coach, open a new chat, paste.
  4. ✅ Check yourself: the day page ends with a "Check yourself" quiz (5 questions; 8 on Fridays; 15 on Day 60). You need your score for the day report.
  5. 📦 Report, then hand in: first click Copy the day report, paste it into a new chat, answer, and save the answer in summary.md in your day work folder. Then drag all the files on the "Hand in" list, summary.md included, into your hand-in folder (section 4).

7. Good to know

  • A normal chat, no special modes: don't turn on Guided Learning in Gemini, Study mode in ChatGPT, or Canvas. The lesson text turns the chat into your tutor by itself.
  • A new chat for every lesson. If a free limit runs out, paste the same text into a new chat with the other tutor (Gemini or ChatGPT).
  • Screenshot: Win+Shift+S, then Ctrl+V into the chat. Clip (Boss days only): Win+Alt+R to start and stop.
  • Reviews: on self-review days the tutor plays a demanding art director. Feedback from Thalia, the course coordinator, waits for you in THALIA-COMMENTS.md (section 4).
  • Credit the makers: OpenStreetMap maps as "© OpenStreetMap contributors"; Wikimedia Commons photos with author and licence; CC-BY packs with the author's name. All of it goes in credits.md, one line per source.
  • A weak PC or an unusual keyboard (no numpad, Alt+Z opens the NVIDIA overlay, Home shows the top view): section 16 and section 17 of ACCOUNTS-AND-INSTALLS.
  • EN / UA at the top of every page switches the language. Progress is saved in the browser: open the course from the same link, on the same computer and in the same browser. Your course page also tells your coordinator which days you opened and how far you got (no name, only your course code), so we can help in time.
  • Never share passwords, and don't buy anything.
